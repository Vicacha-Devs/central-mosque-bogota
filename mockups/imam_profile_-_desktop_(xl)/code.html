Spiritual Journey & Scholarly Background

A graduate of the prestigious Al-Azhar University with a PhD in Islamic Jurisprudence, Sheikh Ahmad has led the Central Mosque of Bogotá for over 15 years. His work focuses on integrating traditional spiritual values with the complexities of modern urban life, fostering a sanctuary for dialogue and growth.
