Our Journey

A Legacy Built on Faith

"The dream was simple: to create a home for the community that reflected both our Islamic heritage and our Colombian identity."

Ahmad Al-Mansour

Founding Member

lightbulb

1995: The Initial Vision

A small group of local residents gathered to envision a permanent space for worship and community growth in the heart of Bogotá.

foundation

2002: Construction Begins

After years of planning and fundraising, the foundation was laid. The project became a symbol of unity, supported by local and international donations.

celebration

2010: Grand Opening

The mosque was officially inaugurated with a ceremony attended by dignitaries and the community, marking a new chapter for Muslims in Colombia.

groups

2020: Community Expansion

Completion of the new community wing, featuring classrooms, a library, and a multi-purpose hall for youth events and social services.
