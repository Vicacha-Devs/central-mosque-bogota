Design Philosophy

This variant honors the unique conical architecture of the Muisca-influenced structure while seamlessly integrating it into a classic Islamic circular emblem. The geometry of the pillars symbolizes the pillars of faith and the community support.

  • architecture

    Local Integration

    The iconic thatch-style cone from the reference image is modernized into a sharp, minimalist geometric triangle.

  • palette

    Heritage Tones

    Combining the colors of the Colombian flag's spirit with institutional deep blue for a timeless, professional identity.

  • history

    Abu Bakr As-Siddiq

    Elegant Naskh-style typography ensures the Islamic identity remains central and respected.
