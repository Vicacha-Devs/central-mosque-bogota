Biography

Sheikh Ahmad Al-Bogotí has served the Bogotá community for over a decade. He completed his advanced studies in Islamic Jurisprudence (Fiqh) and Arabic Linguistics at the prestigious Al-Azhar University.

His mission is focused on fostering interfaith dialogue, community growth, and guiding the youth of Colombia towards a balanced spiritual life. He regularly conducts weekly classes on Tafsir and Islamic ethics.
