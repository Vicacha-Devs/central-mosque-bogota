Sermon Summary

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful...

Patience (Sabr) is not merely waiting; it is how we behave while we are waiting. In today's discourse, we reflect on the trials faced by the prophets and how their steadfastness serves as a beacon for our modern struggles.

The Quran reminds us: "O you who have believed, seek help through patience and prayer. Indeed, Allah is with the patient." (2:153).
