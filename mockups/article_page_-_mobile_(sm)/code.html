The Significance of Ramadan in Bogotá

Imam Ahmed

October 24, 2023

As the sun sets behind the Andes mountains, the call to prayer echoes through the streets of Bogotá. For the local Muslim community, this moment marks more than just the end of the day.

Ramadan in a city high in the mountains brings unique challenges and beautiful opportunities for spiritual growth. The thinner air and the vibrant city life create a backdrop for reflection that is truly distinct to the Colombian capital.

"O you who have believed, decreed upon you is fasting as it was decreed upon those before you that you may become righteous."

— Quran 2:183

The Central Mosque of Bogotá serves as the heart of this experience. During the holy month, the mosque transforms into a sanctuary where cultures from around the world meet. Here, Colombian converts, diplomats from across the globe, and long-standing residents break bread together.

Iftar community gathering at the Central Mosque

Practicing Ramadan here is about building bridges. Whether it's explaining the fast to local neighbors or distributing food to those in need throughout the city, the spirit of charity—Zakat—is felt deeply in every corner of our community.
