Patience in Adversity

Assalamu Alaikum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuh. In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. All praise is due to Allah, the Lord of the Worlds, and peace and blessings be upon the final Messenger, Muhammad, and upon his family and his companions.

Dear brothers and sisters, today we reflect on a virtue that is the backbone of the believer’s character: Sabr (Patience). In our modern world, we are often overwhelmed by the speed of life and the weight of our trials. Yet, Allah reminds us that patience is not merely waiting; it is how we behave while we wait.

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اسْتَعِينُوا بِالصَّبْرِ وَالصَّلَاةِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الصَّابِرِينَ

"O you who have believed, seek help through patience and prayer. Indeed, Allah is with the patient." (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:153)

Consider the life of the Prophets. They faced adversity that we can hardly imagine. From the isolation of Prophet Yunus (as) in the belly of the whale to the immense physical trials of Prophet Ayyub (as). Their strength did not come from their own hands, but from their unshakeable trust in the Divine timing.

In Bogotá, as we navigate our daily lives—whether in our businesses, our families, or our studies—we must remember that every challenge is a tailored test designed to bring us closer to the Creator. When the storm hits, the believer anchors their soul in prayer.

وَبَشِّرِ الصَّابِرِينَ

"And give good tidings to the patient." (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:155)

We ask Allah to grant us a heart that is content with His decree, a tongue that remains moist with His remembrance, and the fortitude to remain steadfast during our darkest hours. May this Ramadan be a transformative journey for all of us.

End of Sermon Transcript - March 24, 2024
