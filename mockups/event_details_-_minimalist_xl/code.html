Annual Community Iftar & Reflection

Join us for a meaningful evening of reflection and community. This event brings together families and individuals from across Bogotá to share in the spirit of togetherness during the holy month.

Our guest speakers will discuss the importance of social cohesion, spiritual growth, and fostering understanding in a modern urban environment. We will break bread together at sunset followed by a collective prayer session and a short keynote address.
