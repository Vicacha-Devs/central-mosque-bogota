Join us for an enlightening Friday Khutbah focused on "The Path to Peace." This session will delve into the spiritual foundations of tranquility in modern times, exploring classical Islamic teachings translated for our contemporary context in Bogotá.

The event is open to everyone. Please arrive 15 minutes early to secure seating in the main prayer hall. Dedicated space is available for sisters.
